# Jordan Gym Reviews — Brand Identity Guide

## Brand idea
A trusted, energetic, Jordan-first guide to gyms, coaches, sports facilities, nutrition services and sports shops. The brand should feel athletic and premium without looking intimidating or medically clinical.

## Positioning and voice
- Promise: discover and compare sports activities with clear information and direct contact.
- Personality: energetic, trustworthy, practical, inclusive, locally rooted.
- Arabic tagline: **دليلك للرياضة في الأردن**
- English descriptor: **Discover. Compare. Get Moving.**
- Voice: concise, helpful, transparent. Label paid placements clearly as **إعلان** or **ممول**.

## Logo direction
Use a compact abstract motion/checkmark mark that suggests movement, progress and a verified listing. Pair it with the wordmark **Jordan Gym Reviews**. Arabic descriptor may be used in campaign material, not forced into the small header logo.
- Primary: full-color mark + wordmark on light backgrounds.
- Reverse: white wordmark and green mark on deep navy.
- Mark-only: app icon, favicon, QR poster and social avatar.
- Clear space: at least the width of the mark's central stroke on every side.
- Minimum sizes: mark 24 px; horizontal wordmark 150 px digitally.
- Never stretch, add bevels, or use the logo over busy photography without a dark overlay.

## Color system
| Token | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Navy | `#071827` | Primary background, premium brand base |
| Forest Surface | `#102B38` | Cards, navigation and elevated surfaces |
| Emerald | `#22C55E` | Primary action, links, active states |
| Lime | `#A3E635` | Small highlights, badges and campaign accents only |
| White Mist | `#F3F7FA` | Primary text on dark backgrounds |
| Cool Gray | `#9FB4C1` | Secondary text, metadata and supporting labels |
| Border | `#294351` | Subtle dividers and outlines |
| Success | `#10B981` | Confirmed/positive state |
| Warning | `#F59E0B` | Expiry and attention state |
| Danger | `#F87171` | Destructive/error state |

Use Emerald for one dominant action per panel. Lime is an accent, not a replacement for accessible text contrast. Never communicate status by color alone.

## Typography
- Arabic: **Cairo** (current app font), weights 400/500/600/700/800.
- English and numerals: **Inter** where available; system sans-serif fallback.
- Headings: bold and compact; body: 14–16 px; metadata: 12–13 px.
- Use tabular numerals for prices, counts, dates and metrics where possible.

## UI system
- Mobile-first, RTL-first, 8 px spacing rhythm, 12–20 px card radii.
- Cards: deep forest surface, subtle 1 px border, restrained hover lift.
- Buttons: emerald primary with dark foreground; outlined secondary; red reserved for destructive actions.
- Inputs: clear labels, visible focus ring, large touch targets (minimum 44 px).
- Ratings: star + numeric score + review count; never rely on stars alone.
- Sponsored listings: persistent explicit “إعلان” or “ممول” label.
- Dashboards: prioritize the next action, then KPIs, then trends and detail tables.
- Activity themes can vary by category, but keep the main logo, spacing, typography and accessibility rules consistent.

## Photography and illustration
Choose authentic Jordanian facilities and people training naturally; show inclusive age and ability ranges, coaches helping members, real equipment and clean facilities. Prefer natural light, dark teal/neutral grading and energetic crops. Avoid exaggerated body transformations, misleading before/after imagery and generic medical stock photos.

## Iconography
Use simple rounded-line icons, consistent 1.75–2 px stroke, minimal fills and recognizable silhouettes. Emoji may appear as friendly category labels but should not replace functional icons in the dashboard.

## Marketing applications
- Social: dark navy background, strong one-line Arabic headline, emerald CTA, real local photography.
- QR poster: large high-contrast QR, “امسح الرمز لزيارة صفحتنا”, short URL fallback and mark-only logo.
- Partner/sponsor placements: clearly marked and visually distinct from organic results.
- App icon: mark-only, centered, high contrast; do not place the full wordmark in the icon.

## Accessibility and implementation
Maintain WCAG AA contrast for normal text where possible; pair color with text/icon; preserve keyboard focus; respect reduced-motion preferences; use descriptive alt text. Keep analytics and personal data private, and never put secrets or personal customer information in public brand assets.
